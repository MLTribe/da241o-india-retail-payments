Payment landscape

Who is gaining, who is losing, and where

The national totals show debit cards shrinking and UPI expanding. This page breaks that change down by issuing bank, by UPI app and by state, and tests whether the shift is broad-based, concentrated, or spreading.

Landscape hypotheses

Supported means the 95% interval (or the sign test) lies on the predicted side. These are descriptive associations across banks, apps and states, not causal effects.

Method

The four kinds of test used here

Shares are tested on the log-odds scale so that a move from 2% to 4% counts like a move from 50% to 67%. Quarterly series use 4-quarter changes and 4 Newey-West lags; monthly series use 12-month changes and 12 lags.

Data: RBI Bank-wise ATM/PoS/Card Statistics (monthly HTML releases, Mar 2022–Mar 2026; Jan–Feb 2022 use an older layout and are excluded here; older-layout all-bank totals from Jun 2014 feed the spliced long card series on the Hypotheses page); NPCI UPI ecosystem statistics by app, member bank and state (Jan 2022–Mar 2026; states from Apr 2023); PhonePe Pulse state-wise quarterly transaction counts (CDLA-Permissive-2.0). NPCI's state series is shown descriptively only, because the share of UPI it cannot assign to a state rose from 13% to 44%. Generated by analysis/landscape_tests.py. See the main hypotheses.
