MDR on UPI P2M above ₹2,000 Policy

Zero since Jan 2020; 0.4% on select P2M payments above ₹2,000 is announced from 15 Oct 2026. The base case is the pre-MDR baseline. A fee makes cards relatively cheaper to accept where they compete with UPI, and creates a new fee pool.
