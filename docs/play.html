What this shows

Credit-card payments can grow even while their share falls.

At this market growth rate, a card can lose up to 23.3% of its growth baseline and still grow.

How is this scenario calculated?

First, every method grows by the market-growth setting. Then the chosen fractions of credit and debit counts after that growth are assigned to UPI. The total stays fixed for that growth setting. These are hypothetical accounting moves, not measured customer switches or estimated effects.

The actual comparison uses NPCI UPI P2M and RBI domestic card-purchase totals for the April–March fiscal years. The three rails may overlap when a card funds a UPI payment, so these are shares of a defined analytical group rather than shares of India's entire retail-payments market. See the data sources.
