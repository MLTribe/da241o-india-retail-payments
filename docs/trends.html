What the series show

    Card lines are card-network payments only. RBI's daily file counts card payments processed by card networks. RBI's monthly Payment System Indicators also count other card payments. In March 2026 the daily file shows about 56% fewer credit-card payments and 9% fewer debit-card payments than the monthly figures. The credit-card gap has widened since 2023, which fits (but does not prove) RuPay credit cards being used through UPI. Use the daily card lines for the timing of changes (weeks, festivals, weekdays); for total card spending, use the monthly figures on Explore the series.
