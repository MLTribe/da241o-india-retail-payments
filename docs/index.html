The question we're asking

As UPI expands, how are other payment methods changing?

We’ll compare changes in transaction volume and each method’s share of a clearly defined group. A payment method can grow in volume while losing share if the whole group grows faster, so we will keep those ideas separate.

How to read this study

What the data can—and can’t—say

This is a descriptive study of public monthly totals, not a test of what caused each payment choice.

1
Growth is not the same as displacement

A rail can gain transactions and still lose share if the comparison group grows faster.

2
The payment types overlap in real life

Some credit-card-funded UPI payments may also appear in card totals. Shares need careful interpretation.

3
National data hide local differences

These figures do not identify a merchant, customer, city, or individual switch between payment methods.

4
Coincidence does not prove cause

Several policy, technology, and economic changes happened at once. We will describe patterns, not claim causation.

Where the numbers come from

Official data, carefully described

We use monthly public statistics from the agencies that publish India’s payment-system and price data.
